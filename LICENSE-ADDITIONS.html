Additional terms and conditions pursuant to Section 7 of the GNU General Public License, Version 3 (GPLv3).

Requires fetchconfig 9.67 or newer. The installed version is read from <FETCHCONFIG_PATH>/fetchconfig/Constants.pm; a warning banner is shown on every page (after login) if it is older than 9.67 or cannot be determined.

Long backups and web server timeouts. While Backup Now or the Empty Backup Cleanup scan runs, a keepalive is sent every 10 s, so the web server's inactivity timeout (Apache Timeout) only needs to be longer than 10 s and BACKUP_TIMEOUT may exceed it. BACKUP_TIMEOUT must be higher than the longest backup run of any device in the device table. A reverse proxy or load balancer in front must not buffer the response; under mod_fcgid, FcgidBusyTimeout must also exceed BACKUP_TIMEOUT.
